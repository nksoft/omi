Official Omi Tournament Rules

1. Setup & Team Matrix

Omi is a 4-player hand-taking game where players sitting opposite each other form a team. South (You) and North (Bot) are partnered against West and East.

2. The Deck & Precedence

The game is played with a 32-card deck (Aces, Kings, Queens, Jacks, 10s, 9s, 8s, and 7s only). Value precedence is strictly: A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7.

3. Dealing & Trump Selection (Thurumpu)

The dealer deals 4 cards counter-clockwise. The player designated as the trump selector chooses the Trump suit based on these 4 initial cards. After selection, the remaining 4 cards are dealt.

4. Hand Gameplay

Counter-clockwise play. Players must follow the lead suit. If they have no cards of the lead suit, they can play any card, including a Trump. The highest card of the lead suit wins, unless a Trump is played, in which case the highest Trump card wins the hand.

5. Tournament Token Scoring

There are 8 total hands in a game. First team to win 5 or more hands wins the round and earns tournament tokens:

  • 5, 6, or 7 hands won by the trump selecting team: +1 token.
  • 5, 6, or 7 hands won by the defending team: +2 tokens.
  • All 8 hands won (Kapothi) by any team: +3 tokens.
  • A tie (4-4): 1st tie rolls over a token to the next round. If two consecutive ties happen, the tie state cancels and resets.
